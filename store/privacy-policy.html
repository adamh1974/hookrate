Hookrate privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

What Hookrate reads

Hookrate is a browser extension for Facebook Reels creators. It only runs on Meta Business Suite (business.facebook.com). When you open the Insights pages there, it reads the numbers on screen: your posts' titles, publish dates, views, viewers, interactions, watch time, 3-second views and your Page's daily follows.

Where that data goes

Nowhere. Hookrate stores these numbers in your browser's extension storage on your own device, so it can show trends over time. Hookrate has no servers, doesn't send your insights to anyone, and doesn't use analytics or tracking. It never asks for your Facebook password and can't post or change anything on your Page.

Payments (Pro only)

If you buy Hookrate Pro, checkout is handled by ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com) and Stripe (stripe.com). They collect your email address and payment details under their own privacy policies. Hookrate never sees your card details. To check whether you have Pro, the extension asks ExtensionPay for your paid status. None of your Reels data is included in that request.

Deleting your data

Removing the extension deletes everything it stored. To cancel Pro, click the "PRO ✓" badge in the Hookrate panel, which opens ExtensionPay's subscription page.

Not affiliated with Meta

Hookrate is an independent tool. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Meta Platforms or Facebook.
